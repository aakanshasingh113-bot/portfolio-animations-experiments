How to play

  • Press on your pen, drag backwards, and let go to flick it.
  • Grab the middle for a straight shot. Grab near an end to make it spin.
  • Knock the other pen off the desk to win the round. Lose your own and you lose it.
  • First to win 3 rounds takes the match.
