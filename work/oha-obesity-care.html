PART 1The problemWhy obesity care fails, who it’s for, and what made it hard.
01 · Why obesity care fails today

Obesity is a medical condition.
Most apps treat it like a diet.

Crash dietsHigh relapse; weight comes back
Manual cites >70% in crash-diet models · source to confirm
Diet-only appsIgnore metabolism, mind and safety
Medicine-onlyPeople don’t trust it on its own
02 · The people it’s for

Adults whose weight is quietly becoming a health risk

Programme target groups
BMI 23 and above (Asian cut-off)Normal weight with belly fatPre-diabetes, fatty liver, high cholesterolSedentary professionalsPost-pregnancyStuck at a plateau
Situations · hypotheses from desk research
Unaware of the riskNeeds a clear, credible score
Tried before, regainedNeeds proof this is different
Told by a doctorNeeds the lab picture
Short on timeNeeds a plan that fits life
03 · The design challenge

A great programme on paper.
Hard to start, hard to stick with.

01Half the score needs tests the user doesn’t have yet
0261 questions before anyone sees value
03Four experts that could feel like four apps
04A health score that must inform, not frighten
how might we…Help people understand their real health risk, choose the right care and keep going for months, safely?
PART 2The programmeWhat users get, and how it helps.
04 · The programme in one picture

A care team,
not a diet plan.

Doctor

Reviews the score, orders tests, clears activity, decides on medicine if needed

Nutritionist

Personal diet plans that change every 15 days, with plateau protection

Mental wellness coach

Emotional eating, stress, habits and relapse prevention

Yoga and fitness trainer

Live group sessions, 5 days a week, with safe progression

Rahul’s care planOne plan, one app, four experts
Body composition scaleHome lab tests8-hour expert chatMonthly reports

Medicine only when a doctor decides it’s needed.
